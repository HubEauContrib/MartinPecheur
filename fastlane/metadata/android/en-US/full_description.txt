MartinPêcheur shows the state of a French river on a map, from public data. Every value carries its date and the name of its source. The app is in French and covers French rivers only.

Four kinds of information, never merged into one:

• Flow: what field agents observed during campaigns, from May to September, on small streams (ONDE network). Between two campaigns, nobody observes these points.
• Discharge: discharge and water level measured by hydrometric stations. A value sent automatically may be shown before any human check. The comparison with historical data is not computed yet.
• Flood vigilance: the level published by Vigicrues for each monitored river section. The app relays it and computes none of its own. It is not an alert system.
• Restrictions: drought alert areas, severity levels and restricted uses at the point you choose, with, when the source provides it, the address of the prefectoral order, which alone is authoritative.

Also: favorites, and filters by flow state, département and discharge freshness.

No account, no ads, no tracker, no server of its own: the app calls the public services Hub'Eau, VigiEau and Vigicrues directly, and IGN for the base map.

Information, not permission
Public Hub'Eau data: indicative, partial, sometimes old, not validated. They do not take dam releases into account. They never replace a prefectoral order, an irrigation decision, or a safety assessment before swimming, boating or crossing.
